Understanding Short Coupon Effects

This chapter demonstrates the critical importance of proper day-count convention handling when dealing with short coupons in fixed-rate bonds using QuantLib.

The Problem

When pricing fixed-rate bonds with Actual/Actual convention, short first coupons require special handling. The bond engine and yield-based pricing can give different results due to how they calculate the accrual period for the first coupon.

Key Issue

Short coupon calculation requires reference dates to determine the regular underlying period, but the curve-based approach doesn't have access to this information.
