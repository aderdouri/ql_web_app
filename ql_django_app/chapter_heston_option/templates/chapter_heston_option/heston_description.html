Mathematical Background

Heston Model Equations

Asset Price Process:

dSt = μSt dt + √Vt St dW¹

Variance Process:

dVt = κ(θ - Vt) + σ√Vt dW²
Model Parameters
  • St: Asset's value at time t
  • μ: Expected growth rate
  • Vt: Variance of the asset
  • θ: Mean reversion level
  • κ: Mean reversion speed
  • σ: Volatility of volatility
  • ρ: Correlation between W¹ and W²

Key Concepts

Stochastic Volatility

The Heston model incorporates time-varying volatility, unlike the constant volatility assumption in Black-Scholes

Mean Reversion

Volatility tends to revert to a long-term mean level, captured by the κ and θ parameters

Correlation

The ρ parameter captures the correlation between asset price and volatility movements
