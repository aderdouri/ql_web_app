Understanding Your Results

Premium vs Discount: If the bond price is above face value, it's trading at a premium (coupon rate > market rate). If below, it's at a discount (coupon rate < market rate).

Clean vs Dirty Price: Clean price is what you see quoted, dirty price is what you actually pay (includes accrued interest).

Financial Terms Glossary

Face Value (Par Value)

The principal amount of the bond that will be repaid at maturity. This is the amount the bondholder receives when the bond expires.

Coupon Rate

The annual interest rate paid by the bond, expressed as a percentage of the face value. This is the regular income you receive from the bond.

Net Present Value (NPV)

The current worth of all future cash flows from the bond, discounted at current market rates. This tells you what the bond is worth today.

Clean Price

The bond price excluding accrued interest. This is the price you typically see quoted in financial markets.

Dirty Price

The bond price including accrued interest. This is the actual amount you pay when buying the bond between coupon payments.

Bond Yield

The annual return you earn on the bond, taking into account its current price, coupon payments, and time to maturity.

Accrued Interest

Interest that has accumulated since the last coupon payment but hasn't been paid yet. This is added to the clean price.

Spot Rate

The current interest rate for a specific maturity period. Used to discount future cash flows to their present value.

Yield Curve

A graph showing interest rates for different maturity periods. Helps determine the appropriate discount rate for each cash flow.
