Chapter Overview

In this chapter we will consider a simple example to model fixed rate bonds. Let's consider a hypothetical bond with a par value of 100, that pays 6% coupon semi-annually issued on January 15th, 2015 and set to mature on January 15th, 2016. The bond will pay a coupon on July 15th, 2015 and January 15th, 2016. The par amount of 100 will also be paid on the January 15th, 2016.

Key Concepts

Fixed Rate Bonds

Bonds that pay a fixed coupon rate at regular intervals until maturity, when the principal is repaid.

  • Regular coupon payments
  • Fixed interest rate
  • Principal repayment at maturity
Yield Curves

A curve showing the relationship between interest rates and time to maturity, used for bond pricing and valuation.

  • Spot rate construction
  • Discount factor calculation
  • Present value computation
Bond Pricing

The process of determining the fair value of a bond using discounting methods and yield curve information.

  • Cash flow discounting
  • Clean vs dirty pricing
  • Yield to maturity
QuantLib Framework

A comprehensive library for quantitative finance that provides tools for bond modeling, pricing, and risk analysis.

  • FixedRateBond class
  • DiscountingBondEngine
  • Schedule generation
