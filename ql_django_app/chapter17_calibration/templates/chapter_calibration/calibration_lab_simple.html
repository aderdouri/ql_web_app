Model Selection
What is model calibration? Calibration finds model parameters that best fit observed market data. Different models capture different aspects of interest rate behavior.
Hull-White

Single-factor model with mean reversion. Best for standard interest rate modeling.

  • • Analytical solutions
  • • Handles negative rates
  • • Fast calibration
Black-Karasinski

Log-normal model ensuring positive rates. Ideal for high volatility scenarios.

  • • Guarantees positive rates
  • • High volatility markets
  • • Regulatory compliance
G2++

Two-factor model with correlation. Advanced modeling for complex derivatives.

  • • Better market fit
  • • Volatility smile
  • • Complex products

Glossary of Terms

Greek Letters & Symbols
  • α (alpha): Mean reversion speed - how fast rates return to normal
  • σ (sigma): Volatility - how much rates fluctuate
  • θ (theta): Long-term rate level
  • ρ (rho): Correlation between factors
  • η (eta): Second factor volatility in two-factor models
Technical Terms
  • Calibration: Finding best model parameters
  • Swaption: Option on interest rate swap
  • RMSE: Root Mean Square Error - measure of fit quality
  • Volatility: Measure of rate uncertainty
  • Mean Reversion: Tendency of rates to return to long-term average
