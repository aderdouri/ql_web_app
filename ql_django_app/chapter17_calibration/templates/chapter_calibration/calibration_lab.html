Parameter Guide

Basic Parameters:
α (alpha): Mean reversion speed
How quickly rates return to normal
σ (sigma): Volatility level
How much rates fluctuate
G2++ Parameters:
β, η (eta): Second factor parameters
Additional factors for complex dynamics
ρ (rho): Correlation between factors
How factors interact with each other
Tip: Think of a thermostat - α controls return speed, σ controls variations, and ρ controls factor interaction.
