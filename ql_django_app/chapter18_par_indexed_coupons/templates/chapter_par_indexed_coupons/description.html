Key Concepts

Par Coupons

In par coupon calculations, the floating rate is calculated over the duration of the coupon period.

  • Rate over coupon accrual period
  • May cause swap value jumps
  • Requires P&L adjustments
Indexed Coupons

In indexed coupon calculations, the rate is calculated over the LIBOR tenor period.

  • Rate over LIBOR tenor period
  • Market convention consistent
  • Requires convexity adjustments
