Understanding the Results
Par Coupons

Par coupons use the rate over the actual coupon period, providing a straightforward calculation method.

  • Simple: Rate × Days in coupon period
  • Direct: No complex adjustments needed
Indexed Coupons

Indexed coupons use the rate over the LIBOR tenor period, which may differ from the coupon period.

  • Complex: Rate × Days in LIBOR period
  • Realistic: Matches market conventions
Key Insights

The difference between methods: Shows how day count conventions and rate application periods affect swap pricing. This is crucial for accurate risk management and pricing in real-world financial markets.
