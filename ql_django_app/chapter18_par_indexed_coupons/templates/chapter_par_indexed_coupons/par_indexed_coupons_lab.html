Parameter Guide

Par Coupons:
Method: Rate calculated over coupon accrual period
Common in textbook examples, may introduce small errors
Characteristics:
• Swap value jumps when coupon rate is fixed
• Requires P&L adjustments
• Uses "wrong rate over right period"
Indexed Coupons:
Method: Rate calculated over LIBOR tenor period
More theoretically correct, requires convexity adjustment
Characteristics:
• Consistent with market fixing methodology
• Requires convexity adjustment for period mismatch
• Uses "right rate over wrong period"
Key Insight: The difference between methods arises from the mismatch between coupon accrual periods and LIBOR tenor periods. Both approaches have trade-offs between theoretical correctness and practical implementation.
