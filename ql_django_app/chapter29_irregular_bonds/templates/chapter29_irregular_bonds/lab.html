Understanding Irregular Bonds

Irregular Fixed: Bonds with missing or null last coupons, requiring special handling.

Fixed-to-Floating: Hybrid bonds that transition from fixed to floating rate payments.

Custom Coupons: Bonds with different indices for different coupon periods.

Financial Terms Glossary

Irregular Bond

A bond with non-standard payment schedules, missing coupons, or mixed rate structures that requires special modeling techniques.

Null Coupon/Gearing

A technique to cancel or eliminate specific coupon payments by setting their rate or gearing to zero.

Fixed-to-Floating Bond

A hybrid bond that pays fixed rate coupons initially, then switches to floating rate coupons for the remainder of the term.

Custom Coupon

A coupon that uses a different interest rate index or calculation method than the standard bond coupons.

Cash Flow Manipulation

The process of modifying, combining, or restructuring bond cash flows to achieve specific payment structures.

Leg Combination

Combining different types of cash flow legs (fixed, floating, custom) to create complex bond structures.
