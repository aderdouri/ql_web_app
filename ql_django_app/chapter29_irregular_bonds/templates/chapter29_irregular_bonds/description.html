Chapter Overview

This chapter explores advanced techniques for building bonds with irregular features that don't fit standard bond structures. Based on real-world questions from QuantLib users, we'll learn how to handle complex scenarios like bonds with missing last coupons, fixed-to-floating rate transitions, and custom coupon schedules.

We'll cover three main scenarios: bonds with null last coupons, fixed-to-floater bonds that transition from fixed to floating rates, and floating legs with short initial stubs using different indices. Each technique demonstrates how to manipulate QuantLib's cash flow structures to achieve the desired bond behavior.

What You'll Learn:
  • Handling bonds with irregular coupon schedules
  • Creating fixed-to-floating rate bonds
  • Custom coupon manipulation techniques
  • Advanced cash flow engineering

Key Concepts

Irregular Coupon Schedules

Bonds where the last coupon date occurs before maturity, requiring special handling to cancel or modify the final coupon payment.

  • Null rate/gearing techniques
  • Cash flow manipulation
  • Custom bond construction
Fixed-to-Floating Bonds

Hybrid bonds that pay fixed-rate coupons initially, then transition to floating-rate coupons for the remainder of the term.

  • FixedRateLeg construction
  • IborLeg integration
  • Cash flow combination
Custom Coupon Engineering

Advanced techniques for creating custom coupons with different indices, rates, and payment schedules to meet specific bond requirements.

  • IborCoupon customization
  • Multiple index handling
  • Pricer assignment
Cash Flow Manipulation

Techniques for modifying, combining, and restructuring bond cash flows to achieve complex payment structures and bond behaviors.

  • Cash flow extraction
  • Leg combination
  • Custom bond creation
