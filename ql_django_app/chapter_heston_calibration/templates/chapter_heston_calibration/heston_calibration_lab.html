Understanding the Calibration Results
Heston Model Parameters
  • θ (Theta): Long-term average volatility level - represents the equilibrium volatility the process reverts to
  • κ (Kappa): Mean reversion speed - controls how quickly volatility returns to its long-term level
  • σ (Sigma): Volatility of volatility - measures the randomness in volatility changes
  • ρ (Rho): Asset-volatility correlation - captures the leverage effect in financial markets
  • v₀ (V0): Initial variance level - starting point for the volatility process
Calibration Quality Assessment
  • Excellent (<3%): Model provides highly accurate pricing
  • Good (3-5%): Model suitable for most trading applications
  • Acceptable (5-10%): Model useful for risk management
  • Poor (>10%): Model may need parameter adjustment
Method Selection Guide
  • Least Squares: Best for smooth, well-behaved problems
  • Differential Evolution: Excellent for complex optimization landscapes
  • Levenberg-Marquardt: Fast but sensitive to initial conditions
