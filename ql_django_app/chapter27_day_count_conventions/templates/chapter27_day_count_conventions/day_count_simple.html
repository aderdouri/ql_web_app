Key Concepts

Day Count Conventions

Different methods for calculating time periods between dates, affecting interest rate calculations and option pricing accuracy.

  • Actual/365 (Fixed)
  • Business/252
  • Impact on pricing
Pricing Engine Issues

When different day-count conventions are used for risk-free rates and volatility, pricing engines may produce inconsistent results.

  • Inconsistent time calculations
  • Pricing discrepancies
  • Engine-specific problems

Day Count Convention Types

Actual/365 (Fixed)

Uses actual calendar days divided by 365, regardless of leap years.

  • • Simple calculation
  • • Common in bonds
  • • Fixed denominator
Business/252

Uses business days divided by 252, excluding weekends and holidays.

  • • Trading days only
  • • Market-based
  • • Excludes holidays
