Key Concepts

Convergence Theory

Monte Carlo methods rely on the law of large numbers to converge to the true expected value as the number of simulations increases.

  • Strong law of large numbers
  • Central limit theorem
  • Rate of convergence
Error Analysis

Understanding and quantifying the statistical error in Monte Carlo simulations is essential for reliable results and risk management.

  • Standard error estimation
  • Confidence intervals
  • Bias and variance

Convergence Properties

Strong Convergence

The simulation converges almost surely to the true value as the number of paths increases.

  • • Guaranteed convergence
  • • Pathwise convergence
  • • Almost sure convergence
Weak Convergence

The distribution of the simulation results converges to the true distribution.

  • • Distributional convergence
  • • Central limit theorem
  • • Asymptotic normality
Variance Reduction

Techniques to reduce the variance of Monte Carlo estimates for faster convergence.

  • • Antithetic variates
  • • Control variates
  • • Importance sampling
