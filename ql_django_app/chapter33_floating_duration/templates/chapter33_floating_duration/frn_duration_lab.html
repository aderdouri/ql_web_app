1. The Problem: Incorrect Duration Calculation

The standard method treats the floating-rate bond like a fixed-rate bond. The coupon amounts do not change when the interest rate varies.

2. Problem Explanation

The standard function fails to update coupon forecasts when the interest rate changes.

What are coupons? Coupons are the regular interest payments you receive from the bond. For floating-rate bonds, these payments change based on market interest rates.

3. The Solution: Curve Linking

The correct approach is to link the forecast curve to the interest rate curve. This way, when the interest rate changes, both the discount factors AND the coupon amounts change.

What are discount factors? These are used to calculate the present value of future payments. A higher interest rate means lower present value (more discounting).
