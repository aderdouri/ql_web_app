Key Concepts

Futures Options

Options on futures contracts have different characteristics than stock options. They are settled in cash and have unique pricing considerations.

  • Cash settlement
  • No dividend adjustments
  • Forward price basis
Black Formula

The Black formula provides an analytical solution for pricing European options on futures contracts, similar to Black-Scholes but adapted for futures.

  • Analytical solution
  • Futures-specific
  • Risk-free rate handling

Pricing Considerations

Forward Price

The expected future price of the underlying asset at expiration.

  • • No arbitrage condition
  • • Risk-free rate impact
  • • Cost of carry
Volatility

The uncertainty in the underlying asset's future price movements.

  • • Historical vs implied
  • • Time to expiration
  • • Market conditions
Time Value

The additional value due to the time remaining until expiration.

  • • Decay over time
  • • Volatility impact
  • • Exercise probability
