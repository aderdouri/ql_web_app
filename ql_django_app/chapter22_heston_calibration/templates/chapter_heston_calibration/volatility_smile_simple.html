Key Concepts

Volatility Smile

The volatility smile is the pattern of implied volatilities across different strike prices, showing that out-of-the-money options have higher implied volatilities than at-the-money options.

  • Implied volatility patterns
  • Market data analysis
  • Surface construction
Heston Model

The Heston model is a stochastic volatility model that allows volatility to change over time, capturing the volatility smile effect in option pricing.

  • Stochastic volatility
  • Mean reversion
  • Correlation effects
