Mathematical Background

Heston Model Equations

Asset Price Process:

dSt = μSt dt + √Vt St dW¹

Variance Process:

dVt = κ(θ - Vt) dt + σ√Vt dW²
Model Parameters
  • V0: Initial variance
  • κ: Mean reversion speed
  • θ: Long-term variance level
  • σ: Volatility of volatility
  • ρ: Correlation between price and volatility

Key Concepts

σ
Volatility Smile

The phenomenon where implied volatility varies with strike price, creating a smile-shaped curve

3D
Volatility Surface

A 3D representation of implied volatility as a function of both strike and time to maturity

⚙
Model Calibration

The process of finding model parameters that best fit observed market prices
