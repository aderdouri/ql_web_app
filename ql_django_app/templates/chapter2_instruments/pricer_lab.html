Pricing Results

How Option Price Changes with Stock Price

This chart shows how much the option is worth at different stock prices. The black line shows your current scenario, and orange lines show what happens when you change other settings.

How to use: First, calculate your option price to create the black line. Then change any setting (like interest rate or volatility) and click "Update Chart" to see the orange line. This shows you how sensitive the option is to different market conditions.

Understanding the Results

Understanding Your Results

The results above show the calculated option price and Greeks based on your input parameters. Each metric provides specific information about how the option behaves under different market conditions.

How to Read the Results:
  • NPV: The fair price of the option today
  • Delta: If the stock goes up $1, the option price changes roughly $X
  • Gamma: Shows how Delta changes if the stock price changes a bit
  • Vega: Shows how the option price reacts if the market becomes a bit more volatile
