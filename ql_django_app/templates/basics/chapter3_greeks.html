Understanding Greeks

Delta (Δ)

Delta measures the rate of change of the option price with respect to changes in the underlying asset price.

  • Range: 0 to 1 for calls, -1 to 0 for puts
  • Hedge ratio for delta-neutral portfolios
  • Highest for at-the-money options
Gamma (Γ)

Gamma measures the rate of change of Delta with respect to changes in the underlying asset price.

  • Always positive for long positions
  • Highest for at-the-money options
  • Measures convexity of option price
Theta (Θ)

Theta measures the rate of change of the option price with respect to time decay.

  • Usually negative for long positions
  • Accelerates as expiration approaches
  • Time value erosion
Vega (ν)

Vega measures the rate of change of the option price with respect to changes in volatility.

  • Always positive for long positions
  • Highest for at-the-money options
  • Volatility risk measure

Calculation Methods

Analytical Greeks

Closed-form solutions for Black-Scholes model.

  • • Fast and accurate
  • • Limited to simple models
  • • No numerical errors
Finite Differences

Numerical differentiation using small perturbations.

  • • Works with any pricing model
  • • Requires multiple pricing calls
  • • Sensitive to step size
Automatic Differentiation

Exact derivatives using computational graphs.

  • • Machine precision accuracy
  • • Works with complex models
  • • Higher computational cost
