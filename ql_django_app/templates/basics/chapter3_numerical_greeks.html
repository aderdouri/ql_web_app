Key Concepts

Numerical Greeks

Greeks measure the sensitivity of option prices to various market parameters. Numerical methods provide accurate calculations for complex instruments.

  • Delta - Price sensitivity
  • Gamma - Delta sensitivity
  • Vega - Volatility sensitivity
Risk Management

Greeks are essential tools for risk management in options trading. They help traders understand and hedge their positions effectively.

  • Portfolio hedging
  • Risk assessment
  • Position monitoring

Greeks Calculation Methods

Forward Difference

Calculate Greeks using forward finite difference approximation.

  • • Simple implementation
  • • First-order accuracy
  • • Suitable for most cases
Central Difference

More accurate method using central finite difference approximation.

  • • Higher accuracy
  • • Second-order accuracy
  • • Better for sensitive calculations

Optimize step sizes for numerical stability and accuracy.
