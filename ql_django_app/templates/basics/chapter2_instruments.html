Key Concepts

Financial Instruments

QuantLib provides a comprehensive set of financial instruments including options, bonds, swaps, and more. Each instrument has specific characteristics and pricing requirements.

  • Instrument constructors
  • Payoff functions
  • Exercise conditions
Pricing Engines

Pricing engines implement different methodologies for valuing financial instruments. Choose the appropriate engine based on your instrument type and market conditions.

  • Analytical solutions
  • Numerical methods
  • Monte Carlo simulation

Pricing Methodologies

Black-Scholes

Analytical solution for European options under the Black-Scholes model.

  • • Fast and accurate
  • • Assumes log-normal returns
  • • Constant volatility
Binomial Tree

Numerical method using discrete time steps to model price movements.

  • • Handles American options
  • • Flexible and intuitive
  • • Can be computationally intensive
Monte Carlo

Simulation-based method using random sampling for complex instruments.

  • • Handles complex payoffs
  • • Multiple underlying assets
  • • Requires many simulations
