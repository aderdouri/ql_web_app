Key Concepts

Model Definition

The Hull-White model describes the evolution of short-term interest rates using a stochastic differential equation.

drt = (θ(t) - art)dt + σdWt
  • Mean reversion to θ(t)
  • Volatility σ
  • Speed of reversion a
Simulation Process

Monte Carlo simulation generates multiple interest rate paths to analyze the model's behavior and properties.

  • Random path generation
  • Statistical analysis
  • Convergence verification

Model Properties

Mean Reversion

Interest rates tend to return to a long-term average over time.

  • • Prevents extreme rate levels
  • • Realistic market behavior
  • • Controllable reversion speed
Term Structure Fit

The model can be calibrated to match current market yield curves exactly.

  • • θ(t) function calibration
  • • Market consistency
  • • No arbitrage conditions
Analytical Solutions

Many derivatives can be priced analytically under this model.

  • • Bond pricing formulas
  • • Option pricing
  • • Fast computation
