Mathematical Background

Heston Model Equations

Asset Price Process:

dSt = μSt dt + √Vt St dW¹

Variance Process:

dVt = κ(θ - Vt) dt + σ√Vt dW²
Available Methods
  • L-BFGS-B: Fast method with parameter bounds
  • SLSQP: Good for constrained problems
  • Nelder-Mead: Simple but reliable method
  • Powell: Works without derivatives
