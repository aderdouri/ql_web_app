Interactive Cap and Floor Laboratory

Welcome to the Interactive Lab! This laboratory allows you to experiment with cap and floor pricing using different parameters and methods.
Lab Features:
  • Parameter Adjustment: Modify strike rates, notional amounts, and dates
  • Pricing Methods: Compare constant volatility vs. volatility surface
  • Real-time Results: See NPV calculations instantly
  • Visualization: Interactive volatility surface charts
Getting Started:
  1. Set your evaluation date and parameters
  2. Choose between constant volatility or volatility surface pricing
  3. Adjust the strike rate and notional amount
  4. Click "Calculate" to see the results
  5. Explore different scenarios and compare results
Note: This is a simplified laboratory for educational purposes. Real-world cap and floor pricing involves additional complexities and market data.
