Method Explanation
{% if results.pricing_method == 'Constant Volatility' %}

Constant Volatility Approach: This method uses a single volatility value ({{ results.constant_volatility_used|floatformat:4 }}) for all caplets in the cap. While simple, this approach doesn't capture the volatility smile or term structure effects observed in real markets.

The Black formula is applied to each individual caplet, and the total cap value is the sum of all caplet values.

{% else %}

Volatility Surface Approach: This method uses market-implied volatilities that vary by strike and tenor. The approach involves:

  1. Constructing a cap/floor volatility surface from market quotes
  2. Stripping individual optionlet volatilities using QuantLib's OptionletStripper1
  3. Pricing each caplet with its specific volatility

This provides more realistic pricing that accounts for market volatility patterns.

{% endif %}
