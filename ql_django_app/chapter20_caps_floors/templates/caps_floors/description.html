Theoretical Background

What are Caps and Floors?

Caps provide protection against rising interest rates by paying the holder when rates exceed a predetermined strike level. Floors provide protection against falling interest rates.

Each cap can be decomposed into a series of caplets, each valued using the Black formula for interest rate options.

Pricing Approaches

Constant Volatility: Uses a single volatility value for all caplets, suitable for simple analysis and educational purposes.

Volatility Surface: Uses market-implied volatilities that vary by strike and tenor, providing more realistic pricing.
