Key Concepts

Interest Rate Caps

Caps are interest rate derivatives that provide protection against rising interest rates. They pay the holder when rates exceed a predetermined strike level.

  • Instrument construction
  • Payoff functions
  • Exercise conditions
Pricing Engines

Pricing engines implement different methodologies for valuing caps and floors. Choose the appropriate engine based on instrument type and market conditions.

Pricing Methodologies

Black-Scholes

Analytical solution for caplets under the Black-Scholes model.

  • Fast and accurate
  • Assumes log-normal returns
  • Constant volatility
Volatility Surface

Market-implied volatilities that vary by strike and tenor.

  • Handles smile effects
  • More realistic pricing
  • Requires market data
Optionlet Stripping

Extract individual caplet volatilities from cap quotes.

  • Handles complex payoffs
  • Multiple underlying assets
  • Requires calibration
