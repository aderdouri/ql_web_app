Key Concepts

Interest Rate Caps

Interest rate caps provide protection against rising interest rates by paying the holder when rates exceed a predetermined strike level.

  • Upward rate protection
  • Series of caplets
  • Strike rate
Interest Rate Floors

Interest rate floors provide protection against falling interest rates by paying the holder when rates fall below a predetermined strike level.

  • Downward rate protection
  • Series of floorlets
  • Minimum rate guarantee
