Key Concepts

Yield Curve Construction

Yield curves are built from market quotes using various interpolation methods. QuantLib provides sophisticated tools for constructing smooth and arbitrage-free curves.

  • Market data interpolation
  • Discount factor calculation
  • Zero rate derivation
Market Quotes

Market quotes represent the current market prices and rates for various financial instruments. These quotes are the foundation for building yield curves and pricing other instruments.

  • Deposit rate quotes
  • Futures price quotes
  • Swap rate quotes

Market Data Types

Deposit Rates

Short-term interest rates for deposits, typically used for the short end of the yield curve.

  • • Overnight to 1 year
  • • Simple interest
  • • High liquidity
Futures Prices

Interest rate futures prices used to derive forward rates for the medium-term curve.

  • • 3-month to 2 years
  • • Forward rate derivation
  • • Convexity adjustment
Swap Rates

Interest rate swap rates for the medium to long-term portion of the yield curve.

  • • 2 to 30 years
  • • Par swap rates
  • • Semi-annual payments
Bond Coupons

Government bond coupon rates used for the long-term portion of the yield curve.

  • • 2 to 30 years
  • • Fixed coupon bonds
  • • Par pricing
