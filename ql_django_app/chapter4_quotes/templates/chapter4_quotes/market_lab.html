Understanding the Results
What Your Results Mean

This section explains what the pricing results above tell you about your bond calculations.

Initial Price Analysis

This represents the fair market value of your 15-year, 4% coupon bond based on the Nelson-Siegel yield curve constructed from your input parameters.

Key Point: This is the theoretical price if all market conditions remain exactly as you've set them.
Final Price Analysis

This shows the bond's value after any changes you made to market prices in the input tables above.

Key Point: This reflects how market price changes affect the bond's valuation through the yield curve.
Price Change Interpretation
Positive Change

Bond is worth more due to favorable market conditions

Negative Change

Bond is worth less due to unfavorable market conditions

No Change

Market conditions had minimal impact on bond value

Practical Interpretation:
For Investors:
  • Positive change = Good investment opportunity
  • Negative change = Consider selling or hedging
  • Large changes = High sensitivity to market conditions
For Risk Management:
  • Monitor yield curve changes regularly
  • Large price swings indicate high risk
  • Use for portfolio rebalancing decisions
