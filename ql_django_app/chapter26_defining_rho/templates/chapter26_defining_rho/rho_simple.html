Key Concepts

Rho Sensitivity

Rho measures how much an option's price changes when interest rates change. It's crucial for understanding interest rate risk in option portfolios.

  • Interest rate impact
  • Portfolio risk management
  • Hedging strategies
Process Differences

BlackScholesProcess and BlackProcess handle interest rates differently, leading to different rho calculations and potential implementation issues.

Calculation Methods

Analytical Rho

Direct calculation using mathematical formulas and derivatives.

  • • Fast computation
  • • Exact results
  • • Process-dependent
Numerical Rho

Calculation using finite differences and perturbation methods.

  • • Process-independent
  • • More robust
  • • Computational cost
