Understanding the Results
Understanding Your Results

The results show the calculated option price and its sensitivities based on your input parameters.

How to Read the Results:
  • NPV: The fair price of the option today
  • Delta: If the stock goes up $1, the option price changes roughly $X
  • Gamma: Shows how Delta changes if the stock price changes a bit
  • Rho: Shows how the option price reacts to interest rate changes
  • Vega: Shows how the option price reacts if the market becomes a bit more volatile
  • Theta: Shows how the option price changes as time passes
