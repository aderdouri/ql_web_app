Understanding Pricing Conventions

This chapter demonstrates the critical importance of consistent day-count conventions when pricing floating rate bonds using QuantLib.

The Problem

When pricing floating rate bonds, we often expect them to trade at par when using a flat rate curve. However, inconsistent day-count conventions between the bond, the interest rate index, and the discounting curve can lead to unexpected pricing results.

Key Issue

Mismatched conventions can cause bonds to trade significantly away from par even when using flat rate curves, leading to pricing errors and arbitrage opportunities.

What You'll Learn

  • Day-count convention mismatches and their impact on bond pricing
  • Three pricing scenarios with different levels of convention consistency
  • Coupon rate analysis showing how conventions affect individual payments
  • Best practices for consistent convention usage in QuantLib
