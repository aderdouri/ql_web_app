Bond Price History Visualization

First Price

-

Initial bond price
This is the bond's clean price at the start of the simulation period. It represents the market value excluding accrued interest.

Last Price

-

Final bond price
The bond's clean price at the end of the simulation period. Compare with the first price to see overall performance.

Min Price

-

Lowest price reached
The lowest price during the simulation period. This indicates the maximum potential loss if you had to sell at the worst time.

Max Price

-

Highest price reached
The highest price during the simulation period. This shows the maximum potential gain if you sold at the best time.

Average Price

-

Mean price over period
The arithmetic mean of all prices during the period. This gives you the typical price level around which the bond traded.

Total Points

-

Number of data points
The number of business days in the simulation period. More points mean more detailed price history and better statistical reliability.

Price Change

-

Total price change
The absolute and percentage change from first to last price. Positive values indicate price appreciation, negative values indicate depreciation.

Volatility

-

Price volatility measure
Standard deviation of price changes. Higher values indicate more price fluctuation and risk. Lower values suggest more stable pricing behavior.
