Chapter Overview

This chapter demonstrates advanced option pricing techniques for both European and American options using QuantLib Python. We'll explore different pricing methods, their convergence characteristics, and the mathematical foundations behind each approach.

What You'll Learn:
  • European option pricing methods
  • American option pricing techniques
  • Convergence analysis and comparison
  • Early exercise optimization

European Options

European options can only be exercised at expiration, making them easier to price analytically.

Pricing Methods:
  • Black-Scholes-Merton: Analytical pricing formula
  • Binomial Tree: Discrete-time lattice model
  • Monte Carlo: Stochastic simulation method

American Options

American options can be exercised at any time, requiring more sophisticated numerical methods.

Pricing Methods:
  • Binomial Tree: Optimal exercise boundary
  • Finite Differences: PDE-based pricing
  • Least Squares Monte Carlo: Advanced simulation

Mathematical Foundations

Black-Scholes PDE

$$\frac{\partial V}{\partial t} + \frac{1}{2}\sigma^2 S^2 \frac{\partial^2 V}{\partial S^2} + rS \frac{\partial V}{\partial S} - rV = 0$$

American Option Constraint

$$V(S,t) \geq \max(S-K, 0) \text{ for calls}$$

$$V(S,t) \geq \max(K-S, 0) \text{ for puts}$$
