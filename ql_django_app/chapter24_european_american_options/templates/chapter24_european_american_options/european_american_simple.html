Key Concepts

European Options

European options can only be exercised at expiration. They have analytical solutions under the Black-Scholes model and serve as benchmarks for numerical methods.

  • Exercise only at maturity
  • Black-Scholes formula
  • Analytical Greeks
American Options

American options can be exercised at any time before expiration. They require numerical methods like binomial trees or finite difference schemes.

  • Early exercise rights
  • Numerical pricing
  • Optimal exercise boundary

Pricing Methods

Black-Scholes

Analytical solution for European options under constant volatility.

  • • Fast and accurate
  • • European options only
  • • Closed-form solution
Binomial Tree

Numerical method using discrete time steps for both European and American options.

  • • Handles American options
  • • Convergence to Black-Scholes
  • • Flexible and intuitive
Finite Difference

PDE-based method for complex option structures and early exercise.

  • • High accuracy
  • • Complex boundaries
  • • Computational intensive
