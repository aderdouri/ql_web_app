Key Concepts

European Options

European options can only be exercised at expiration. They are priced using the Black-Scholes formula.

American Options

American options can be exercised at any time before expiration. They require numerical methods for pricing.

Pricing Methodologies

Black-Scholes

Analytical solution for European options

  • • Fast and accurate
  • • Assumes log-normal returns
  • • Constant volatility
Binomial Tree

Numerical method for both European and American options

  • • Handles American options
  • • Flexible and intuitive
  • • Can be computationally intensive
