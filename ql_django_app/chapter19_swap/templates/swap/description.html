Key Concepts

Swap Mechanics

Interest rate swaps involve the exchange of fixed and floating interest rate payments between two parties over a specified period.

  • Fixed leg payments
  • Floating leg payments
  • Net settlement
Pricing Methods

Swap pricing involves discounting future cash flows using appropriate discount factors derived from the yield curve.

  • Present value calculation
  • Forward rate estimation
  • Par swap rate

Swap Types

Vanilla Swaps

Standard interest rate swaps exchanging fixed for floating rate payments.

  • • Fixed vs floating
  • • Single currency
  • • Standard conventions
Basis Swaps

Swaps exchanging two different floating rate indices.

  • • Floating vs floating
  • • Different indices
  • • Basis risk
Cross-Currency Swaps

Swaps involving different currencies with principal exchange.

  • • Multi-currency
  • • Principal exchange
  • • FX risk
